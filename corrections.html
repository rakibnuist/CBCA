Misinformation Monitoring & Correction

CBCA may formally notify a consultancy when a public claim appears inaccurate, incomplete or materially misleading.

Correction workflow

Document the exact claim

Save the URL, screenshot, publication date, account name and complete surrounding context.

Verify against primary evidence

Compare the claim with the official university, scholarship authority, Embassy or other relevant source.

Internal review

CBCA records the issue, rates its potential impact and checks for conflicts of interest.

Official notice and response period

The publisher receives an email describing the concern, evidence and requested correction, with a reasonable opportunity to respond.

Outcome

The record may be closed after correction, clarified through a joint note, escalated under member rules or addressed through a carefully worded public advisory.

Fairness rule: CBCA should correct information, not attack people. Public wording must be proportionate, evidence-based and distinguish allegation, disputed fact and established finding.

Useful evidence

  • Post or video URL
  • Full screenshot with date
  • Official source link or document
  • Explanation of the contradiction
  • Potential harm to students
