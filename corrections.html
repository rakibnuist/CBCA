Corrections & Public Advisories

A searchable register of documented misinformation cases, with due process and a right of reply for every organization named.

Fair wording standard. CBCA statements identify the exact claim, the evidence and the current verified position. They do not use abusive language, encourage harassment, speculate about motive or declare criminal guilt. Preferred formulations are "incorrect," "unsupported," "misleading," "not verified," "under clarification" and "no evidence supplied by the deadline."

The official notice process

Evidence capture

Screenshot, post URL, date, platform, exact claim, the primary source used to check it and the potential student harm.

Conflict-free review

A reviewer with no conflict of interest confirms the primary source and approves the case level.

Confidential notice

CBCA sends a private notice by email identifying the claim, the evidence, the correct information and a response deadline.

Right of reply

The organization may provide its source, explanation, correction link or objection.

Correction recorded

Where corrected, CBCA records it and may request a visible correction note if the original claim reached students materially.

Publication if unresolved

Where uncorrected or repeated, CBCA may publish a factual clarification after due-process review. For members, the case may proceed to warning, suspension or termination.

When a public advisory may be justified

  • A material false claim remains public after formal notice and a reasonable correction opportunity.
  • A serious pattern affects multiple students or creates immediate risk.
  • A member is suspended or terminated and the public must be able to verify status.
  • A university policy materially changed and prior public claims are now unsafe.
  • An authority issues a relevant official warning or decision.

Right of reply

A named organization may submit a concise response and supporting evidence. CBCA publishes or fairly summarizes a relevant response unless it is abusive, exposes personal data, is clearly unrelated or creates legal or safety risk. New verified evidence triggers review and correction.
