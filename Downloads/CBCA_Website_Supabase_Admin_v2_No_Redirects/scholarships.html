Scholarship Policy Transparency

CBCA will publish the complete terms of Chinese university scholarship policies—including conditions that may be restrictive, unclear or unfavourable to students.

Coverage

Tuition, accommodation, stipend, insurance, application charges, deposits and all known mandatory costs.

Continuation

Academic-performance rules, attendance, annual review, language requirements and conditions for renewal or cancellation.

Student impact

Refund rules, late-payment risks, accommodation limitations, arrival deadlines and policy changes affecting students.

Transparency database

Verified university-policy records

Every published record should show its source, verification date and uncertainty level. Attractive claims must never be separated from restrictive terms.
