Governance responsibility

The Expert Committee approves policy, membership decisions, disciplinary outcomes, high-impact public advisories, annual plans and major institutional commitments.

Conflict-of-interest rule

Any member whose consultancy, employee, business partner, close relative or direct competitor is materially involved in a case must disclose the conflict and withdraw from the decision.
