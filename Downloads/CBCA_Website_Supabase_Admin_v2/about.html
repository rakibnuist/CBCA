About CBCA

An independent professional alliance working to make Chinese education information in Bangladesh more transparent, verifiable and accountable.

Founding motivation

Why CBCA is being established

Bangladeshi students increasingly seek higher education opportunities in China, yet they often struggle to verify scholarship coverage, university policies, application requirements, consultancy authenticity, fees, refund terms and promotional claims.

CBCA is being established by a group of Bangladesh-based China education consultancies to create a credible industry alliance that builds professional standards, verifies members, publishes reliable public information, protects students and represents shared institutional interests.

CBCA’s defining position: a trusted public gateway for Chinese education information—not an admissions agency, processing centre or commercial student-service provider.

Vision

To become Bangladesh’s most trusted gateway to Chinese education information and the country’s leading professional alliance for authentic, accountable and reliable China-focused education consultancies.

Mission

To publish accurate scholarship and application information; document the full terms of Chinese university scholarship policies; promote Chinese education through public programs; verify and strengthen responsible member agencies; challenge misinformation; review student complaints; and build lawful, transparent cooperation with Chinese diplomatic, educational and government stakeholders.

Core principles

Accuracy before speed

Public claims should be based on official or otherwise verifiable evidence.

Transparency before promotion

Unfavourable scholarship terms must be disclosed alongside benefits.

Due process

No agency or individual should be publicly condemned without evidence review and a fair right of reply.

Institutional integrity

CBCA will never claim Embassy, CSC or university authorization without written proof.
