Privacy, Accuracy & Legal Disclaimer

How CBCA handles public information, email submissions, member records and legal boundaries.

Information accuracy

CBCA aims to publish accurate, source-based information. Scholarship policies, university requirements and official procedures may change. Every time-sensitive record should display its source and last verification date.

No consultancy relationship

Using this website, reading a guide or emailing CBCA does not create a student-consultancy, agency, legal or fiduciary relationship. CBCA does not provide personalized admission or visa services.

Member-directory limitation

Membership indicates that an organization satisfied CBCA’s standards at the most recent review. It does not guarantee a particular result, refund, admission, scholarship, visa or future conduct.

Email privacy

This static website does not directly store form submissions. Its forms create a draft in the visitor’s own email application. Complainants should avoid sending unnecessary passport copies, financial credentials, passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

Complaint fairness

A complaint is an allegation until evidence is reviewed. CBCA should provide a right of reply, record conflicts of interest and avoid public identification unless justified under an approved advisory policy.

External links

Links to universities, authorities and social platforms may change. External content remains the responsibility of its publisher.

Launch checklist

  • Approve legal identity and constitution
  • Activate official email accounts
  • Verify founding-member profiles
  • Approve privacy and retention policy
  • Approve complaint and correction teams
  • Publish source and update dates
