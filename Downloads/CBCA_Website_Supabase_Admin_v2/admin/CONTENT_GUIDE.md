# CBCA Content and Publishing Rules

## Never publish
- Unverified scholarship claims or copied agency claims presented as official.
- “Official Embassy Partner”, “CSC Authorized” or equivalent wording without written authorization.
- Private student identity or complaint evidence without a lawful and approved basis.
- A public accusation before evidence review and right of reply.
- Incomplete member data presented as verified.

## Every scholarship-policy record needs
1. Primary source.
2. Publication date and CBCA verification date.
3. Full financial coverage and all known charges.
4. Renewal, cancellation and refund terms.
5. Clear distinction between fact, interpretation and unresolved uncertainty.

## Every correction notice needs
1. Exact challenged claim.
2. Archived evidence.
3. Primary contradictory source.
4. Internal conflict check.
5. Written notice and response deadline.
6. Final outcome and authorized approver.

## Every complaint record needs
1. Case reference.
2. Intake date and scope decision.
3. Evidence index.
4. Right-of-reply record.
5. Conflict declarations and recusals.
6. Outcome, follow-up and retention date.
