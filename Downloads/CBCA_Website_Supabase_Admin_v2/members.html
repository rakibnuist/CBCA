Application and verification

New agencies may apply by email. Admission is not automatic. CBCA may review business records, office existence, operating history, reputation, public claims, complaint history and willingness to follow the member code.

Your email application will open in your device’s mail app. Attach trade licence, office evidence and other requested documents manually.
