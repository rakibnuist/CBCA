University Policy Transparency Database

The complete available terms of Chinese university scholarships — not only the attractive benefits.

Policy commitment

CBCA publishes the complete available terms of Chinese university scholarships. Where a policy is restrictive, unclear, financially burdensome or potentially harmful to students, CBCA publishes the relevant facts, the source and a student-impact explanation in a neutral, evidence-based manner.

Founding-phase rule. CBCA publishes objective fact matrices and student-impact flags. It does not publish numeric rankings or "best/worst" lists. Any future rating system would require written criteria, expert review, appeal rights and formal approval.

Source hierarchy

CBCA ranks evidence in this order. Lower-ranked material is treated as a lead for verification, never as final authority.

Official regulation or webpage

Current official university, CSC, Embassy or government webpage or regulation.

Official document

Official brochure, admission notice, scholarship agreement or official portal text.

Direct written response

Email or written response from an authorized university office.

Redacted student document

Award letter, invoice or scholarship agreement used only as corroboration.

Informal posts — leads only

Agency social-media posts or informal messages used only as leads for verification.

Adverse or sensitive publication protocol

  1. Record the exact policy text, source, date and potential student impact.
  2. Check whether a newer official source changes the position.
  3. Send a concise clarification request to the university where a material issue is unclear or disputed.
  4. Publish facts and status — not emotional labels such as "fraud university" or "bad scholarship."
  5. Include the university's response, or note that no response was received by the stated date.
  6. Correct the page and preserve a visible change log when new evidence appears.

Database status

Pilot phase

The policy database is being built university by university. Published records appear in the scholarship records table. Records are only published once an official source has been identified and dated.

Request a clarification

If you have an official document that conflicts with a published record, send it with the source link. CBCA will review and correct promptly where the evidence supports it.
