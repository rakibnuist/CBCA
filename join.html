Apply for CBCA Membership

Submit your consultancy's application below. Admission is not automatic — every application is reviewed against CBCA's professional standards.

Before you apply. CBCA may review business records, office existence, operating history, public reputation, prior complaints and willingness to follow the member code of conduct. Incomplete or inaccurate applications may be rejected.

How the review works

Submit your application

Complete the form with accurate consultancy, representative and contact details. Submission is not an admission decision.

Acknowledgement and reference

The Secretariat assigns an application reference and confirms receipt by official email.

Initial screening

The Membership Review & Audit Team checks completeness, legal identity, experience and that the consultancy is an active, legitimate business.

Verification and office visit

Reviewers may request trade licence copies, office evidence, references or clarification, and may conduct a physical office inspection.

Documented recommendation

The team issues a recommendation: approve, provisionally approve, defer, reject or request remediation.

Decision and appeal

The authorized committee decides and sends the outcome by official email. A rejected or deferred applicant receives a concise written reason and may appeal with new evidence.

Minimum eligibility

  • Permanent, identifiable office suitable for professional operation.
  • At least three years of relevant operational experience, unless the committee formally approves a different founding-phase category.
  • Good reputation supported by reasonable evidence and absence of unresolved serious misconduct.
  • Truthful advertising and willingness to correct inaccurate content.
  • Transparent written fees, refund terms and student agreements for your own services.
  • Commitment to respond to CBCA complaints and compliance notices.
  • Consent to periodic review and annual information renewal.

Already a member? Use the contact page to update your consultancy's public record rather than submitting a new application.

What membership means

  • Public listing in the verified member directory
  • Right to display the CBCA trust mark while in good standing
  • Agreement to the member code of conduct
  • Accountability under the complaint and correction process
  • No guarantee of admission, scholarship or visa outcomes
  • No student leads or referrals from CBCA

Evidence you may be asked for

  • Trade licence / registration evidence
  • Office lease or ownership evidence and dated photographs
  • Owner and representative identification
  • Website and social-media ownership evidence
  • Signed code of conduct and data declaration
  • Complaint and disciplinary history declaration
