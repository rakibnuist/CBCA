Privacy, Data Protection & Disclaimer

What CBCA collects, why it is collected, how it is protected and the limits of everything published on this site.

Privacy principles

  • Collect the minimum information necessary for membership, complaints, events or communications.
  • State purpose, access, retention and disclosure before collection.
  • Do not collect student academic profiles for referrals or services.
  • Redact passports, IDs, offer letters, visas, financial details and personal conversations before publication.
  • Do not publish complainant identity without informed consent or a compelling lawful basis.
  • Allow correction of inaccurate personal or member directory data.

Security

  • Domain, hosting, DNS and recovery access registered to CBCA-controlled accounts.
  • SSL, regular updates, spam controls and secure backups.
  • Role-based administrative access, multi-factor authentication and audit logs.
  • Automated backup with periodic restore testing.
  • Incident-response plan for data breach, account compromise and impersonation.

General disclaimer

CBCA is a non-political, non-profit professional alliance in its founding phase. It is not a court, police authority, government regulator or legal representative, and it does not claim Embassy, CSC, university or government authorization unless written evidence of that authorization is published.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data CBCA holds about you, subject to legal and record-retention obligations. Members may request correction of inaccurate directory data at any time.
