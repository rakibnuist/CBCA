Governance authority

  • The Expert Committee approves policy, membership decisions, disciplinary outcomes, public advisories of high reputational impact, annual plans and major institutional commitments.
  • The President provides strategic leadership and authorized external representation.
  • The General Secretary controls official correspondence, agendas, minutes, resolutions and records.
  • The Treasurer controls approved accounts, receipts, budgets and financial reporting.
  • The IT, Website & Social Media Secretary controls digital infrastructure subject to access-sharing and audit rules.
  • The Emergency & Crisis Coordinator coordinates urgent incidents, but cannot alone declare guilt or publish punitive statements.

Conflict of interest and recusal

Any person whose consultancy, employee, business partner, close relative or direct competitor is materially involved in a complaint, membership review, misinformation case or policy dispute must declare the conflict and withdraw from the decision. The recusal is recorded in the minutes.

Meetings and records

  • At least one general meeting each month during the founding phase.
  • Agenda circulated by official email before the meeting where reasonably possible.
  • Minutes record attendees, conflicts, evidence reviewed, decisions, responsible persons and deadlines.
  • Verbal, WhatsApp or WeChat decisions have no formal effect until confirmed through official email or approved minutes.

Delegated teams

Membership Review & Audit Team

Reviews new-member applications: checks completeness, identity, experience, office and reputation, conducts site verification and issues a documented recommendation.

Roster pending appointment

Scholarship Policy Transparency & Research Unit

Collects official scholarship sources, maintains the policy data sheet and change log, requests clarification from universities and publishes neutral fact matrices.

Information Verification & Correction Desk

Monitors public claims, captures evidence, checks claims against primary sources, contacts publishers and maintains the correction register.

Student Protection & Complaint Resolution Desk

Receives complaints, checks jurisdiction and urgency, assigns case references, requests written responses and facilitates fair resolution.

Email-only formal record

Membership applications, complaint cases, correction notices, committee approvals, institutional proposals and official decisions are handled through organization-controlled email. Messaging apps may be used for alerts, but the formal record is confirmed by email.
