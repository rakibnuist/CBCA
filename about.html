About CBCA

An independent public-information, professional-standard and member-verification alliance for Bangladesh-based China education consultancies.

Founding motivation

Why CBCA was established

Bangladeshi students increasingly seek higher education opportunities in China, but the market contains both responsible agencies and operators that publish incomplete, inaccurate or misleading information. Students and families often struggle to verify scholarship coverage, university policy, application requirements, consultancy authenticity, fees, refund terms and the truth of promotional claims.

CBCA was established by a group of Bangladesh-based China education consultancies to create a credible industry alliance: an organization that builds professional standards, verifies its members, publishes reliable public information, protects students and represents shared interests before relevant institutions.

Official positioning. CBCA aims to become the trusted public gateway for Chinese education information in Bangladesh — not an admissions agency, not a processing center and not a commercial student-service provider.

Vision

To become Bangladesh's most trusted gateway to Chinese education information and the country's leading professional alliance for authentic, accountable and reliable China-focused education consultancies.

Mission

To publish accurate scholarship and application information; document the full terms of Chinese university scholarship policies; promote Chinese education through public programs; verify and strengthen responsible member agencies; challenge misinformation; review student complaints; and build lawful, transparent cooperation with Chinese diplomatic, educational and government stakeholders.

Our public promise

  • Verified public information, not private consultancy.
  • Transparent scholarship and university-policy data, including restrictive or unfavorable conditions.
  • Clear application-process and documentation guidelines that students can follow independently.
  • A verified member directory and accountable professional standards.
  • Evidence-based correction of misleading agency claims.
  • A fair complaint-review, mediation and escalation pathway.
  • No claim of Embassy, CSC or university authorization without written evidence.

The absolute non-service boundary

CBCA does not provide student services. CBCA will not accept or process a student application; assess an individual profile; choose a university for a student; prepare, translate, attest, verify or submit documents; create an account; write an SOP or study plan; communicate with a university on behalf of a student; arrange admission, scholarship, visa, travel or accommodation; collect a student service fee; assign a student lead; refer a student to a particular member; or guarantee any result.

What CBCA publishes for everyone

  • General scholarship announcements summarized from verifiable official sources.
  • Step-by-step public application guides any student can follow independently.
  • General document checklists and explanations of common document types.
  • Official deadlines, eligibility, coverage, renewal conditions and policy changes.
  • Public webinars, seminars, pre-departure briefings and awareness campaigns.
  • A searchable directory showing current verification status of member consultancies.
  • General fraud-prevention, fee-transparency and student-rights information.

Relationship with member agencies

Member consultancies remain independent commercial entities and may provide their own services under their own agreements, fees and legal responsibility. CBCA membership indicates that an agency satisfied CBCA's verification standards at the last review date; it does not guarantee admission, scholarship, visa, refund, service quality or future conduct.

Financial separation

  • CBCA does not receive student consultancy, application or processing fees.
  • CBCA funds come only from approved membership fees, lawful donations, sponsorships, event support or other formally approved non-student-service sources.
  • No member may collect a private service payment using CBCA's name, bank account, receipt or letterhead.
  • Any sponsor relationship affecting published content must be disclosed.

No unauthorized status claims. CBCA may seek cooperation with the Chinese Embassy, CSC, Chinese universities and relevant authorities. Until written recognition or authorization is obtained, CBCA does not describe itself as an "Embassy partner," "CSC representative," "official scholarship authority," "government-approved body" or equivalent.

Core values

How we work
