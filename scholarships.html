Read before you apply

Student-impact flags we publish

These are the conditions most likely to cost a student money or a place if they are discovered late.

Partial tuition

The scholarship does not fully cover tuition, or covers it only for certain years or programs.

Accommodation gaps

Accommodation is not covered, only partially covered, or restricted to a specific dormitory type.

Unclear stipend

Stipend amount, start date or payment schedule is not clearly published.

Non-refundable fees

A mandatory deposit or fee is non-refundable, or the refund rule is unclear.

Strict renewal

Annual renewal conditions are strict, vague or applied at university discretion.

Cancellation risk

The scholarship may be reduced or cancelled without a clearly published warning or appeal process.

Shifting language rules

Language requirements may change after admission or before graduation.

Opaque selection

Application channel, quota or selection method is not transparent.

Conflicting wording

Published promotional wording conflicts with the detailed official policy.

Verification status

What each status label means

Verified — officialSupported by a current official webpage, brochure, regulation or direct official email.
Partially verifiedSome material terms confirmed; others unclear or unavailable.
Under clarificationCBCA has asked the university or authority to clarify a material issue.
Conflicting informationTwo credible sources state different terms; both are disclosed.
OutdatedSource relates to a previous intake or has not been reconfirmed.
Student report — unverifiedA redacted report is recorded but not yet independently confirmed.
