Public Application Guidelines

General, source-aware information students can use independently. CBCA does not assess profiles, prepare files or submit applications.

Scope. These are public educational guidelines, not personalized advice. Requirements vary by scholarship, university, province, program and application year.

Common application routes

Identify the official route

Confirm whether the opportunity is applied for through an Embassy or government route, a scholarship portal, a university portal, a provincial program or a direct university process.

Read the full policy

Check eligibility, program language, tuition coverage, accommodation, stipend, deposits, insurance, renewal standards and refund terms — not only the headline benefit.

Verify the institution and source

Use the official university or scholarship authority website. Treat screenshots, agent posts and copied brochures as secondary evidence until confirmed.

Prepare documents independently

Follow the exact checklist and format requested. Never submit altered, fabricated or misleading academic, financial, employment or identity documents.

Keep a complete record

Save submitted files, portal screenshots, payment evidence, emails, application numbers and official responses.

Typical document categories

Identity

Passport, photograph and any identity declarations required by the official application.

Academic

Certificates, transcripts, grading explanations, study plans, research proposals or recommendation letters where required.

Language

Chinese- or English-language evidence accepted by the specific institution and program.

Compliance

Medical, police, financial, guardianship or other declarations where officially required.

Documentation principles

  • Understand what each document is and who normally issues it.
  • Distinguish an official requirement from a practical suggestion.
  • Treat legalization, notarization, medical, police-clearance and language requirements as jurisdiction- and program-dependent.
  • Redact names, passport numbers, QR codes and identifiers before sharing any sample document.

Always verify current requirements. Scholarship rules and university procedures change. Check the publication and verification dates on any CBCA record, then confirm against the official source before you act.

Protecting yourself from misleading claims

  • Any promise of "guaranteed" admission, scholarship or visa
  • Any claim of Embassy, CSC or university authorization without written evidence
  • Pressure to pay quickly, in cash, or without a written receipt
  • Refusal to provide a written service agreement with itemized fees and refund terms
  • Requests to submit altered or fabricated documents

CBCA will not

  • Evaluate an individual profile
  • Select universities for a student
  • Prepare or translate documents
  • Submit applications
  • Process admission or visa files
  • Assign leads to member agencies
  • Receive student service fees
