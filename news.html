Editorial standard

What we publish and how we verify it

Every public post carries source metadata. CBCA channels exist to distribute verified public information — not to generate leads or promote private consultancy services.

Source identified

Each factual claim states its source or source category.

Dated

Publication date and, for policy content, the last-verified date.

Status labelled

Verified, partially verified, under clarification, conflicting or outdated.

Exclusions stated

Material exclusions and conditions — not only promotional benefits.

Non-service disclaimer

Included wherever a post could be read as an offer of help.

Corrections visible

CBCA corrects its own errors promptly, with a note stating what changed and why.

Press

Media enquiries

Journalists and institutions may request comment, data or an interview through the media mailbox. CBCA provides evidence-based releases, public advisories and correction logs, and identifies an authorized spokesperson for each matter.

media@cbcabd.org

Follow official channels

Announcements are published here and on CBCA's official Facebook page. Treat information attributed to CBCA from any other source as unverified until you can find it on an official channel.
