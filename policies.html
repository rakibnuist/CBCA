Member code of conduct

  • Publish only claims supported by current evidence, and identify material conditions or limitations.
  • Never guarantee admission, scholarship, visa or government approval.
  • Never claim to represent a university, the Embassy, CSC or CBCA beyond written authority.
  • Use written service agreements, itemized fees and clear refund terms.
  • Protect student data and never post unmasked personal documents without explicit consent.
  • Respond promptly to CBCA verification, correction and complaint communications.
  • Correct material errors visibly — not by silently editing or deleting a post.
  • Do not misuse CBCA's name, logo, certificate, email, letterhead or position for personal advantage.

CBCA trust mark

The CBCA trust mark is a revocable indication of current membership status. It may appear on approved member websites, office displays and official promotional materials only according to the brand-use guide, and it must point to the member's live verification page or membership number.

Prohibited uses

  • "CBCA guarantees our service," "CBCA-approved scholarship," "Embassy-approved through CBCA," or similar unsupported wording.
  • Displaying the badge after suspension, expiry or termination.
  • Placing the badge on a specific scholarship claim in a way that implies CBCA endorsement.
  • Using committee titles in private disputes, collections or unrelated commercial negotiations.

Disciplinary outcomes

Finance and conflict of interest

Permitted funding: approved membership fees, lawful donations or grants, transparent event sponsorship and institutional support, and approved publication or training activities that do not constitute student consultancy services.

  • Student application, processing, scholarship, document or visa charges collected by CBCA.
  • Undisclosed payment in exchange for favorable scholarship or university-policy coverage.
  • Use of CBCA accounts for a member's private transaction.
  • Cash collection without an approved receipt and record.

Published factual findings are not altered because a university, agency or sponsor objects without evidence.

Report a trust-mark misuse

If you see the CBCA mark or name used with a guarantee claim, or displayed by a suspended organization, report it with a screenshot and link.
