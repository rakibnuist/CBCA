What CBCA cannot answer

CBCA does not respond to requests for profile evaluation, university selection, application preparation, visa processing or private consultancy support. Students who send such requests receive a standard non-service response directing them to the public guides and the verified member directory. CBCA does not recommend, rank, assign or privately route a student to any particular consultancy.
