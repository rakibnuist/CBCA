Student Protection & Complaint Desk

Evidence-based complaint review with due process, right of reply and clearly stated limits on what CBCA can do.

CBCA's authority and its limits. CBCA is not a court, police authority, government regulator or legal representative. It may review evidence, request responses, facilitate a fair settlement, recommend voluntary refund or restitution, discipline members and help a complainant identify an appropriate authority. CBCA cannot compel a non-member to participate, guarantee recovery or declare criminal liability.

What you can complain about

CBCA may receive complaints concerning member and non-member student consultancies where the matter relates to alleged misrepresentation, non-delivery, unauthorized payment, refund dispute, document misuse, privacy breach, fake affiliation, forged or suspicious documents, harassment or other serious professional misconduct.

How to submit a complaint

Send it to the official complaints address

Complaints must go to the official complaints email. Sensitive evidence must never be sent to personal accounts or public messaging groups.

Include the essentials

Full name, contact details, agency name, a concise chronology, the amount involved, the agreement, receipts and relevant messages.

Protect your identity documents

At initial intake CBCA asks only for the last four digits of a passport — never the full document unless it is genuinely necessary later.

Receive a case reference

You will receive an acknowledgement and case reference. Acknowledgement does not mean the allegation has been proven.

Review procedure

  1. Log the complaint, assign a reference and secure the evidence.
  2. Check conflicts of interest and assign a review team.
  3. Assess jurisdiction, urgency, evidence and requested remedy.
  4. Send the complaint summary to the concerned party and request a written response.
  5. Compare agreement, receipts, communications, official policy and both parties' explanations.
  6. Offer mediation where appropriate and lawful.
  7. Record outcome, commitments, deadlines and proof of compliance.
  8. Recommend member discipline or referral where the evidence and policy support it.
  9. Send a written closure notice to the parties.

Possible outcomes

Complaint outcome categories.
OutcomeMeaning
ResolvedParties completed an agreed remedy.
Partially resolvedSome issues were remedied; others remain.
No member breach establishedEvidence did not establish a CBCA rule violation.
Insufficient evidenceA responsible conclusion could not be reached.
Non-cooperationThe concerned party did not respond or provide requested evidence.
ReferredMatter directed to an authority, lawyer, university or other competent body.
WithdrawnComplainant withdrew; serious public-risk evidence may still be retained and reviewed.

Confidentiality

Complaint identities and documents are confidential by default. CBCA may publish anonymized statistics. Naming a person or agency requires due process, evidence, necessity and authorization under the public-advisory rules. Personal documents remain redacted.

📢 শিক্ষার্থী ও অভিভাবকদের অবগতির জন্য

চায়না বাংলাদেশ কনসালটেন্সি অ্যালায়েন্স (CBCA) শিক্ষার্থীবান্ধব, স্বচ্ছ ও জবাবদিহিমূলক উচ্চশিক্ষা পরামর্শ পরিবেশ গড়ে তুলতে প্রতিশ্রুতিবদ্ধ। কোনো স্টুডেন্ট কনসালটেন্সির মাধ্যমে অর্থ আত্মসাৎ, মিথ্যা ভর্তি/স্কলারশিপ/ভিসার প্রতিশ্রুতি, ভুল তথ্য, চুক্তি ভঙ্গ, ডকুমেন্টের অপব্যবহার, গোপন ফি, হয়রানি বা গুরুতর পেশাগত অসদাচরণের শিকার হলে প্রমাণসহ CBCA-কে লিখিত অভিযোগ জানানো যাবে।

CBCA অভিযোগ গ্রহণ করে প্রমাণ পর্যালোচনা করবে, সংশ্লিষ্ট পক্ষের লিখিত বক্তব্য চাইবে, ন্যায়সংগত সমাধানের জন্য মধ্যস্থতার চেষ্টা করবে এবং সদস্য প্রতিষ্ঠানের ক্ষেত্রে গঠনতন্ত্র অনুযায়ী ব্যবস্থা সুপারিশ করতে পারবে।

CBCA কোনো আদালত, পুলিশ, সরকারি নিয়ন্ত্রক সংস্থা বা আইনজীবী নয়; অর্থ ফেরত, ক্ষতিপূরণ বা আইনি ফলাফল নিশ্চিত করতে পারে না। অভিযোগ প্রমাণিত হওয়ার আগে কোনো পক্ষকে দোষী বিবেচনা করা হবে না।
