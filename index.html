Our trust model

CBCA is not a consultancy. It is an accountability layer.

Membership should help students identify authentic agencies—but it is never a guarantee of admission, scholarship, visa, refund or future conduct.

For students and families

Check the source before you trust the claim.

Scholarship advertisements often omit renewal rules, required deposits, accommodation limitations, performance conditions or refundable and non-refundable costs. CBCA’s transparency system is designed to show the complete policy—not only the attractive headline.
