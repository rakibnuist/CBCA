Our trust model

CBCA is not a consultancy. It is an accountability layer.

Membership should help students identify authentic agencies — but it is never a guarantee of admission, scholarship, visa, refund or future conduct.

01Verify member organizations and records.

For students and families

Check the source before you trust the claim.

Scholarship advertisements often omit renewal rules, required deposits, accommodation limitations, performance conditions or non-refundable costs. CBCA's transparency system is designed to show the complete policy — not only the attractive headline.

  • Full coverage and exclusions stated separately
  • Application fees, deposits and hidden costs disclosed
  • Renewal and cancellation conditions published
  • Every record carries an official source and verification date

Need to report harm?

Students may submit evidence of fraud, financial disputes, forged documents, hidden terms, misinformation or serious professional misconduct.

CBCA reviews evidence, requests a written response from the concerned party and facilitates fair resolution. CBCA is not a court, police authority or regulator and cannot guarantee refund or compensation.
