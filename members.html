Seen a badge you doubt? A suspended, expired or former member must not display a current CBCA trust mark. If you see the CBCA mark used with a guarantee claim such as "CBCA-approved scholarship" or "Embassy-approved through CBCA", that use is prohibited — please report it.

New membership

Application and verification

New agencies may apply online. Admission is not automatic. CBCA may review business records, office existence, operating history, reputation, public claims, complaint history and willingness to follow the member code of conduct.

  • Permanent, identifiable office suitable for professional operation
  • At least three years of relevant operational experience
  • Good reputation and no unresolved serious misconduct
  • Truthful advertising and willingness to correct inaccurate content
  • Transparent written fees, refund terms and student agreements

Ready to apply?

Complete the online application form. It goes directly to the Membership Review & Audit Team — no attachments needed to get started.

There is no application fee, and submitting an application does not guarantee membership.
